Who We Are

About Shotric International

Founded in 2021, Shotric International is a sports equipment manufacturer and exporter based in Sialkot, Pakistan — the world's leading sports manufacturing hub. We specialize in combat sports equipment, fight gear and sportswear, manufacturing directly for brands, distributors and private-label businesses worldwide.

Our manufacturing capabilities span boxing equipment, MMA gear, Muay Thai equipment, protective gear, sportswear and gym accessories — all available with custom branding, OEM and ODM services.

We work directly with brands, Amazon sellers, gym equipment distributors, martial arts academies and retailers around the world, providing factory-direct pricing and full customization flexibility.
