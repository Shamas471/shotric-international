How to Order from Shotric International

Our transparent, step-by-step manufacturing process ensures your custom sports equipment is produced exactly to your specifications and delivered on time.

1

Inquiry & Initial Discussion

Send us your requirements via WhatsApp, Email, or our RFQ form. Tell us about the products you need, estimated quantities, and your target market.

What to include: Reference images, tech packs (if available), material preferences, and customization details (logos, colors).
2

Quotation & Specifications

Based on your details, our team will provide a detailed quotation including unit prices, estimated shipping costs, and lead times. We'll finalize the exact specifications of the product with you.

3

Sample Production

Once the quote is agreed upon, we highly recommend producing a physical sample. This ensures the design, materials, fit, and finish meet your exact standards before mass production.

4

Sample Approval & Contract

You receive and test the sample. If any revisions are needed, we adjust accordingly. Once you approve the final sample, we finalize the bulk order contract and production schedule.

5

Bulk Production & QC

After receiving the initial deposit, bulk production begins. We keep you updated throughout the process. Our Quality Control team inspects the products at multiple stages to ensure consistency with the approved sample.

6

Shipping & Export

Upon completion and final quality check, we share photos/videos of your order. Once the final balance is settled, we dispatch your goods via your preferred shipping method (Air, Sea, or Courier).

Policies & Terms

Sample Policy

Samples are fully customizable and charged upfront (including shipping). This cost is often adjusted or credited back when you place a bulk order that meets our MOQ requirements.

Payment Terms

Standard terms are 50% advance deposit to begin production, and the remaining 50% balance prior to dispatch. We accept international wire transfers (T/T), Western Union, and MoneyGram. [Subject to specific agreement]

Shipping Options

  • Courier (DHL, FedEx, UPS): 5-7 days. Best for samples and urgent small orders.
  • Air Freight: 7-12 days. Cost-effective for medium-sized orders.
  • Sea Freight: 3-6 weeks. Most economical for large, bulky orders.

Ordering FAQ

Can you copy a design from another brand?

We can manufacture products inspired by or structurally similar to reference items, but we respect intellectual property and will not illegally counterfeit patented designs or registered logos.

Do you offer packaging design?

Yes, we offer fully customized packaging solutions including branded polybags, mesh bags, custom printed cardboard boxes, and woven labels.

How do I know the quality will be good?

That is why the sample stage is crucial. You physically inspect the sample before mass production begins. We then guarantee that the bulk order will match the approved sample's quality.
