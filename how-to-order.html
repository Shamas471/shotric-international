Simple Process

6 Steps from Inquiry to Delivery

We've designed our ordering process to be as smooth and transparent as possible for international buyers.

01

Send Your Inquiry

Contact us via the quote form, WhatsApp or email. Share your product type, quantity, branding needs and any reference images.

02

Technical Discussion

Our production team reviews your requirements and discusses materials, colors, sizes, branding placement and pricing options.

03

Sample Production

A pre-production sample is manufactured for your review. You inspect the sample, give feedback, and approve before bulk production begins.

04

Bulk Production

Production begins after your approval and deposit payment. We keep you updated throughout the manufacturing process.

05

Quality Control

Multi-stage quality inspection at every step — materials, stitching, branding, padding and final packing are all verified.

06

Export & Delivery

Products are packed and shipped to your location worldwide via your preferred freight method with full export documentation.

Getting Started

What to Include in Your Inquiry

The more detail you provide, the faster we can give you an accurate quote and timeline.

Product Type

Which product do you need? (e.g. boxing gloves, MMA shorts, tracksuits, protective pads)

Quantity

How many units do you need? This affects pricing and production timelines.

Target Timeline

When do you need the products? This helps us plan production and sampling schedules.

Branding Requirements

Your logo files, brand colors, label placement preferences and any branding guidelines.

Reference Images

Any reference products, competitor items or design mockups that show what you're looking for.

Target Price

If you have a target FOB price in mind, share it. This helps us recommend the right materials and specifications.
