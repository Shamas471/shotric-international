Frequently Asked Questions

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Our MOQ varies by product line, but generally starts at 50-100 units for fully customized gear. For specific products, please contact us for exact details.

Do you provide samples before bulk production?

Yes, we strongly recommend a sample run before bulk production. Sample costs and shipping are paid upfront, and can often be credited towards your bulk order.

How long does manufacturing and shipping take?

Sample production usually takes 7-14 days. Bulk production takes 3-5 weeks depending on order size. Shipping times vary by destination and chosen method (Air Freight vs Sea Freight).
