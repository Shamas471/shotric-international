What is OEM vs ODM

OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer)

You provide complete brand specifications. We manufacture to your exact requirements. Your logo, your colors, your packaging.

ODM (Original Design Manufacturer)

You share your product idea or reference. Our team develops specifications and manufacturing approach. Ideal for new brands.
