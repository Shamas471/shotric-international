About Shotric International

Sports equipment manufacturer and exporter — building brands worldwide from the heart of the world's sports manufacturing hub.

Our Story

Shotric International is a sports equipment manufacturer and exporter based in Sialkot, Pakistan — the world's leading sports manufacturing hub.

We specialize in combat sports equipment, fight gear and sportswear, manufacturing directly for brands, distributors and private-label businesses around the world.

Manufacturing Capabilities

Boxing Equipment

MMA Gear

Grappling gloves, shin guards, MMA shorts, and rash guards designed for maximum mobility and durability in the cage.

Muay Thai Equipment

Why Sialkot?

Sialkot, Pakistan produces over 70% of the world's surgical instruments and a significant portion of global sports equipment. The city's manufacturing ecosystem provides unmatched expertise, skilled labor and supply chains for sports goods production, making it the ideal location for global sports brand manufacturing.
