About Shotric International

Sports equipment manufacturer and exporter — building brands worldwide from the heart of the world's sports manufacturing hub.

Our Story

Founded in 2021

Founded in 2021, Shotric International is a sports equipment manufacturer and exporter based in Sialkot, Pakistan — the world's leading sports manufacturing hub.

We specialize in combat sports equipment, fight gear and sportswear, manufacturing directly for brands, distributors, Amazon sellers and private-label businesses around the world.

Our manufacturing capabilities span boxing equipment, MMA gear, Muay Thai equipment, protective gear, sportswear and gym accessories — all available with custom branding, OEM and ODM services.
